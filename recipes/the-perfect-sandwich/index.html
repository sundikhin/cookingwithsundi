Game day / sandwich

the perfect sandwich

This is just my ideal sandwich. A little more elevated than a basic ham and cheese, but still accessible enough for the average person. We sometimes call this a "football sandwich" in my house, because I used to pick up the ingredients at Trader Joe's on Sunday mornings while running errands, then assemble it when I got home, typically in time for a football game. The ingredients won't be measured here, because sandwiches are always measured with the heart.

10 min makes 3 sandwiches no cooking

ingredients

Tick things off as you go.

you'll need

  • Bread knife
  • Small bowl

method

  1. slice the bread

    Slice the baguette lengthwise, and cut it into your desired sandwich lengths. I typically cut my baguette into thirds.

  2. make a pesto aioli

    Mix equal parts pesto and mayo. I prefer Kewpie. This creates a bit of a pesto aioli. Spread it on both halves of the bread.

  3. layer it up

    Layer prosciutto, fresh mozzarella slices and arugula to your heart's content.

a note from sundi

You can technically use any lettuce, but the pepperiness of arugula pairs super well with salty prosciutto and the creamy fattiness of mozzarella.
