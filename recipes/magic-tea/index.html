Sick day / tea

magic tea

Fresh ginger, a cinnamon stick and a handful of cloves, boiled into a spicy, warming tea. What I make whenever I feel a cold coming on.

25 min makes 3–4 mugs big batch

ingredients

Tick things off as you go.

method

  1. prep the ginger

    Peel and slice the ginger root.

  2. fill the pot

    Add 3–4 cups of water to a pot with the cinnamon stick, cloves and sliced ginger.

  3. boil

    Bring it to a boil and let it boil for 20 minutes.

  4. strain and serve

    Strain into mugs and stir in honey to taste.

a note from sundi

This is the tea I make whenever I'm sick or feel a cold coming on. Ginger is incredible for a sore throat or a cough, and cinnamon and cloves have anti-inflammatory properties. I love making a big batch and serving it up with honey, especially in the colder months.
